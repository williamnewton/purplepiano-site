Made for the very small

Big keys, big colors, big sounds. Nothing to read, nothing to buy, nothing to break.

  • No wrong notes

    The keys are tuned to a pentatonic scale, so every combination sounds musical. Mashing the whole keyboard with both hands still sounds lovely.

  • Draw a tune

    In the song maker, kids drag a finger across the squares to draw a melody that loops forever. The keys light up as it plays so they can follow along. MAGIC invents a new singable tune with one tap.

  • Twist the knobs

    Five big sound knobs — Space, Wobble, Sparkle, Echo and Dream — plus four tones: Soft, Mellow, Buzzy and Chunky. Every twist changes the sound instantly, and every pitch keeps its own rainbow color.

  • Safe by design

    No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no internet. Purple Piano works completely offline and collects no data whatsoever. Hand over the iPad without a second thought.

Every C is always pink

Each pitch has its own fixed color, everywhere in the app — on the keys, in the song maker, in the glow when a note plays. Long before they can read music, kids start to see it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 August 2026

Purple Piano collects no data.

That is the whole policy. Everything below just says it more slowly.

What we collect

Nothing. Purple Piano does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data whatsoever.

  • No accounts. There is no sign-up, no login, and no profile.
  • No analytics. We do not measure taps, sessions, crashes, or anything else.
  • No advertising. There are no ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking. We do not track you across apps, websites, or devices.
  • No network requests. The app never contacts a server. It works entirely offline.

What stays on the device

Tunes made in the song maker, along with sound settings such as knob positions and the selected tone, are saved only on your device. They are never uploaded and are never visible to us or anyone else. Deleting the app permanently deletes them.

Children's privacy

Purple Piano is designed for young children. Because the app collects no data at all, there is no personal information from children — or from anyone — for us to gather, store, or disclose. There are no ads, no in-app purchases, no external links, and no way to communicate with anyone through the app.

Third parties

Purple Piano contains no third-party SDKs, analytics libraries, or advertising networks. Nothing is shared with anyone, because nothing is collected in the first place.

This website

This site has no analytics, no tracking scripts, and no cookies. It loads a web font from Google Fonts; apart from that, it makes no external requests.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. If Purple Piano ever begins collecting any data — it has no plans to — that change would be described here before it took effect.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Get in touch via the support contact listed on the Purple Piano App Store page, or see the support page.
